How the status is decided: Present = checked in and checked out. Half Day = went out on a toilet or lunch break and never came back in or checked out. Absent = never checked in. No check-out = checked in but no check-out was scanned. Staff can change any status manually, and every change is saved in the Status Log sheet.

Change the staff PIN

The same PIN is used on every page: the scanner, the live monitor, this tracker and the walk-in desk tools. After you change it, everyone must use the new PIN. Use 6 or more characters with no spaces.

Forgot the PIN? Open the Apps Script project, choose the function resetStaffPin and click Run. The starting PIN written in the code (STAFF_PIN) works again.
